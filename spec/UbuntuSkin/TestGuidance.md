# UbuntuSkin test guidance

## Visual and interaction checks

- In Window Manager, compare the light title bar, centered dark title, three gray discs and thin lower edge with the Writer frame. Open a second window, switch activation, disable a caption action and check the backdrop colors. Maximize and inspect the two overlapping restore squares at actual size. Each horizontal stroke should end at a corner. Restore, resize, minimize where available and close. [Window reference](../../screenshots/LibreOfficeReference/MenuBarAndDropdownMenu.png).
- Compare the white surfaces of TextList, ListView and TreeView. Hover one row while another is selected and check that both fills reach their edges. Hold the mouse button down on a row and check that its gray fill also reaches every edge. Release it and change selection with the keyboard. Verify a white polygon tick in a square checkbox and a white dot in an orange radio. Open the ListView combo and compare the inset on all four sides of its list. Sort a column in both directions and open a grid column filter. [Files row states](../../screenshots/UbuntuReference/TrashBin_FirstItemPressed_SecondItemSelected.png).
- In TextBox, compare the white multiline box with the document box and the gray single-line field. Focus the single-line field and select part of a word. Check the peach outline and dark letters on the peach selection. In both document editors, check the white document surface, rectangular popup, gray menu hover, muted shortcut text and submenu arrow. Hover a style swatch in the ribbon and in the expanded gallery. Check the gray fill and the dark tooltip with light text. [Entry reference](../../screenshots/UbuntuReference/TrashBin_TextBox_FocusedAndPartialSelected.png), [Tooltip reference](../../screenshots/LibreOfficeReference/ToolBarHighlighted_Tooltip.png).
- Move the pointer onto a resting toolstrip or ribbon command. Its face and border should fade directly to their hover colors. Press and latch a command, then open a dropdown and the arrow half of a split button. Confirm the split button's primary action remains reachable. Switch from DarkSkin to Ubuntu with the ribbon expanded, then narrow the window until the toolbar uses three rows and a group collapses. Check the toolbar background and the collapsed button's hover and open colors. Repeat the skin switch with the window narrow, then widen it. Check the thin gray line under a resting tab, the thicker line on hover and the orange selected line. Focus the selected tab with the keyboard and verify the peach top and sides meet the orange indicator without a peach line across the bottom. [Split button reference](../../screenshots/LibreOfficeReference/ToolBar_Split_Highlighted.png), [Tab reference](../../screenshots/LibreOfficeReference/VerticalTag_SecondSelected_ThirdHighlighted.png).
- In Layout / Repeat, drag both scale handles and page the scrollbars. Inspect the orange fill and gray remaining track at zero, halfway and maximum. In DatePicker, open both compact combos and select a day from the current and adjacent months.
- Check default button, indicator, tab and combo dimensions against the previous UbuntuSkin at the same window size and display scale. Switch to another skin and back, then recheck the edits, selected rows and calendar date.
